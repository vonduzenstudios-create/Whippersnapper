# Design decisions

These were agreed with the user during planning. Don't change them without asking.

## Content rules

- **Everything is generated by code.** Sprites, tiles, 3D backdrops, UI, the font, sound effects and music are all made at runtime. The project contains no image or audio files. Keep it that way.
- **All content is original.** The hero, enemies, boss, story, names and music are our own designs, made in the spirit of the genre. Do not use characters, names, melodies or art from any existing franchise. "Castlevania Clone" is only the folder name and never appears in the game.

## Decision table

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Stack | Vite + TypeScript + three.js |
| Look | Pixel sprites in front of lit 3D backdrops (2.5D). Internal resolution 384×224, scaled up with crisp pixels |
| Level shape | A mountain-pass prelude (5 rooms) leading to a connected castle (14 rooms) with an ability gate before the first boss. Behind the Wyrm, the castle opens up into the Undervault and three interconnected regions (changed 2026-10-02, see below). The old Sealed Reliquary test room is gone |
| Expansion (2026-10-02) | The game is a small slice of a much larger one: **no ending and no final boss**, just regular bosses. Exploration is the point: regions loop into each other and back into the old castle through hidden paths and one-way shortcuts, not a simple fork. Rewards are placed where you can see them but need a later ability, so returning pays off. Regions: **Undervault** (hub, behind the Lair), **Kennel Yards and Briar Garden** (wild dogs, boss Gnarlfang, gives the Frostrunner Greaves), **The Belfry** (harpies and bell wraiths, boss the Bell-Mother, gives the Raven Mantle), **Rimeworks Aqueduct** (frost crawlers, gated by both relics, holds the sealed Black Door to a future area). Either boss can be fought first. Save altars in every region |
| Prelude | Opens with a cinematic: an avalanche chases the hero through the snowy Hollowcrown Pass, then buries the way back. Signposts teach the basic controls on the way to the drawbridge (added 2026-10-01 at the user's request) |
| Playable characters | Character select after NEW GAME: **Vesna Korder** or her twin brother **Anzel Korder**. Cosmetic only (same moves and hitbox). Story text fills in the name and son/daughter through tokens in `story.ts` (added 2026-10-02) |
| Run | A default ability (added 2026-10-02). Since 0.2.0: 1.5× walking speed with fading afterimages, and armor counts as 0 while running (risk for the speed). Since 0.10.0 it's held on RT (Left Shift) instead of a double tap. The backdash is unchanged for now, but the user finds it slow and wants it reworked later |
| Shield (0.10.0) | An off-hand equipment slot; the main hand is always the whip. Hold LT to raise the shield: 15% walking speed (since 0.11.0), can jump and crouch, turns with you. Up + a direction aims it diagonally, Up alone holds it overhead (covering straight down, changed by the user in 0.11.0); while aiming up she stands still and left/right only aim. The diagonal pose leans its top back (\ facing right). It cuts melee (weapon strikes and deliberate lunges) by the shield's block value (20% for the starting Wooden Buckler, up to ~70% late) and stops physical projectiles. Body contact and magic/elemental hits ignore it and knock it down for a cooldown. A magic shield may come later |
| New gear (0.10.0) | New games start with the Wooden Buckler. The better shields, the Rootstone (no knockback) and the Throwing Knife (weak, fast, rapid; marks a non-boss, unshielded enemy so its next hit ignores half its DEF) exist, but **the user will place them in the world** |
| Enemy testing (0.10.0) | The old #lab is replaced by test chambers launched from the enemy editor (any enemy or variant, plus bosses, which the editor also lists) |
| Difficulty select (0.2.0) | After character select: Normal / Hard / Brutal = 1× / 1.5× / 2× enemy damage, changeable under Pause → Options. Enemy HP is not scaled (no damage sponges). Mostly a testing aid for tuning, and may go away later |
| Outdoor skies (0.2.0) | No invisible ceilings. Outdoor rooms you can jump high in get an extra map cell of open sky above them, with secret ledges, breakable walls and minor upgrades (Might Seed STR+1, Ward Seed CON+1) |
| Tides (0.2.0) | Aqueduct water rises and falls on a cycle instead of sitting in floating pools. You can swim up (Jump in water) and leap out at the surface, so high tide reaches places a jump can't |
| Story | Vesna (or Anzel) follows their mother, Ilsabet Korder, who climbed to the keep nine days earlier to check the Wyrm's chains. Lore stones carry Ilsabet's notes. After the boss, her letter reveals the Wyrm was only "the Warden" of a door leading deeper. All text lives in `src/game/story.ts` |
| Movement relics | Raven Mantle (double jump) and Frostrunner Greaves (crouch + dash to slide under one-tile gaps). Boss rewards in the expansion (Bell-Mother and Gnarlfang), and they gate the Aqueduct, secrets in the old castle and future areas |
| RPG depth | Medium: HP, hearts, XP and levels, STR/CON/LCK, whip/armor/2 accessories, potions |
| Hero | Vesna or Anzel Korder, chain-whip hunters. Whip aims in 8 directions (SCIV-style), and holding attack after a strike dangles the chain |
| Difficulty | Classic-hard: knockback on every hit, spike pits deal damage and respawn you at the last safe spot |
| Bosses | The Ossuary Wyrm, a segmented bone serpent that burrows through the walls. Below 50% HP it catches fire and gains a perch-and-breathe-fire attack. Expansion: **Gnarlfang**, a two-headed hound that charges, leaps and howls in its pack at half HP. Since 0.3.0 it has two forms: a flayed, vein-covered brute that seems to die, then rises as the Cinder Mastiff (black hide, veins of fire, a burning skull for a second head) with a second HP bar and fire attacks. **The Bell-Mother**, a harpy matriarch who swoops and sends out bell shockwaves. Since 0.3.0 (user's choice) she is a gaunt ash-grey harpy crone with a hooked beak-like face, nude from the hips up (breasts and nipples shown), with ragged plumage from the hips down. Overall tone: very dark, adult horror |
| Experimental relics and enemies (0.4.0) | The user wants to experiment before designing more of the map, so these exist but are **not placed in the world yet**: the Duskwing Shroud (hold Jump to glide), the Undertow Pearl (full speed and free swimming in water), the Ashglass Ward (an activated shield bubble that costs 3 hearts and blocks every monster hit for 3 s), and five enemies: Hookjaw, Mire Eel, Gloomcaster, Rot Ooze, Grave Grub. They're tried in the `#lab` test room. Where each relic is found and what it gates is still open |
| New enemies | Wild Dog (pack runner that pounces), Harpy (swooping flyer that drops feather darts), Bell Wraith (slow ghost whose toll sends out a shockwave), Frost Crawler (crawls on walls and ceilings, drops onto you) |
| Music | Original gothic baroque rock: harpsichord, organ, distorted bass, guitar lead, choir, drums |
| Controls | Keyboard and gamepad, both rebindable. No touch controls |
| Setting | Snowy mountain pass and ice cave, then the drawbridge and castle entrance, then down into flooded catacombs and the boss lair |
| Ability unlock | Chain Grapple: whip an iron ring to latch on, then swing, climb the chain and launch |
| Sub-weapons | Holy Water (1 heart) and Boomerang Cross (2 hearts) |
| Extras | Auto-map with explored %, save altars using localStorage, title/intro/victory/game over screens, damage numbers, hit-stop, screen shake, particles |
| After the boss | The victory screen returns to play instead of the title, and the Lair's far gate opens into the Undervault |
